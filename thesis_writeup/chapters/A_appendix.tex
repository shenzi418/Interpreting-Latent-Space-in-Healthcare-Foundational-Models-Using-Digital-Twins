\section{Floor and anchor supplements}
\label{app:floors}

The full $\alpha$ grid behind Table~\ref{tab:alpha} (24 values of $\alpha$ per encoder and mode, with $\Delta$floor intervals, covering arcs and cyclic-order flags) is the artifact \texttt{tmp\_t4\_alpha\_grid.txt} in the audit archive; the compact table in the main text quotes its \texttt{medalonly}/\texttt{target} rows verbatim. Two supplements are stated here. Label-free $\alpha$ selection rules (generalised cross-validation; group-disjoint source-only cross-validation) place all twelve encoder$\times$mode cells significantly below the constant floor, while target-label-based selection places all twelve above it with only one preserving anatomical cyclic order: the window that clears the floor is reachable only with labels from the evaluation domain. And re-ranking the 24 possible assignments of the four anchor angles to the four real territory classes, each against its own constant floor, places the identity assignment between 7th and 11th of 24 depending on mode: better than most arbitrary assignments, and far from uniquely correct. \tracenote{tmp_t4_alpha_grid.txt, tmp_t4_supplement.txt (12/12 label-free below floor; 12/12 target-oracle above, 1/12 order); anchor ranks MR A.7 item 3 (9-11/24 source, 7/24 target)}

\section[Sweep and alignment details]{Lead-permutation sweep and alignment details}
\label{app:sweep}

Sweep details supporting \S\ref{sec:res-align}. The identity cell scores macro-F$_1$ 0.260, with bootstrap interval $[0.216, 0.301]$ and label-permutation $p=0.114$ at $n=438$. The two transpositions outside that interval lie above it (II$\leftrightarrow$aVR 0.319, aVR$\leftrightarrow$aVF 0.320). Random permutations differ from transpositions on the kernel distance (Mann--Whitney $p=3.9\times10^{-7}$), while the distance does not track damage within transpositions ($\rho=-0.146$, $p=0.20$). The per-cell table is the artifact \texttt{leadperm\_sweep.json}. \tracenote{Card F; outputs/analysis/leadperm_sweep/leadperm_sweep.json}

The nullspace-projection protocol of \S\ref{sec:res-align}: iterative rank-one removal with a balanced logistic domain head, iteration budget 50 and stopping accuracy 0.55; version~1 fits on MedalCare-train plus \ptbxl-test ($n=14{,}217$), version~2 symmetrically on the two training pools ($n=29{,}437$) with both test splits held out; the held-out linear C2ST after projection is 0.963 and 0.969 respectively. Two exclusions referenced in the main text: a label-shift lower-bound argument leaves slack $+0.073$ between the bound and the observed joint error, so label shift does not explain the gap; and the removed directions carry no clinical or biophysical signal, the excess $R^2$ against the six-feature baseline peaking at $-0.069$ and against $\thetavec$ at $-0.024$ (territory) with all components negative. \tracenote{dossier B §2.4 (protocol); Card G item 8 (JS 0.145 bits, floor 0.072 vs observed 0.145; excess R2 all negative)}

\section[Labels and scaler modes]{Territory label design and scaler modes}
\label{app:labels}

The \ptbxl\ four-class territory rule of \S\ref{sec:data-ptbxl} in full. The fourteen MI-class SCP statements split into anterior-pure \{AMI, ASMI, INJAS\}, anterolateral \{ALMI, INJAL\}, inferior-pure \{IMI, INJIN\}, inferolateral \{ILMI, INJIL, IPLMI\}, lateral-pure \{LMI, INJLA\} and excluded-posterior \{PMI, IPMI\}. Records with any posterior code, with both anterior- and inferior-side codes, or with lateral codes only are dropped. Anterior-side records become Anterolateral if an anterolateral or lateral code is present, and Anteroseptal otherwise; symmetrically on the inferior side. On the simulator side, the folder-derived labels are capped by a structural ceiling: \texttt{LCX\_0.3\_ant} and \texttt{LCX\_0.3\_post} share one $\phiang$ wedge, so any folder-label readout is bounded at macro-F$_1$ 0.864 with Inferolateral recall pinned at 0.5, which is why the $\phiang$-derived label is used throughout. \tracenote{A§1.2.4 (:101-159); A§1.1.7 (:120-142)}

The P1 scaler modes, from the analysis code's own documentation. \texttt{source} reuses the MedalCare-train scaler unchanged; the code documents it as a defect for the feature arm, the domains disagreeing on per-column spread by up to threefold. \texttt{target} fits on the label-selected evaluation rows: transductive and unlabelled, but conditioned on the selected subset. \texttt{target\_pool} fits on the full unselected split. \texttt{target\_pool\_measured} fits missing-value-aware statistics on the un-imputed pool; it is equivalent to \texttt{target\_pool} for the latent arm and corrects, for the feature arm, the fact that most pooled feature rows are imputation constants. The P2 modes are \texttt{source} (strict transport, no target information) and \texttt{target} (diagonal correlation alignment on unlabelled target statistics). \tracenote{Card J verbatim docstrings; A§5.1 :451-533}
