\section{Use of Generative AI}
As AI tools are increasingly used for research especially in CS/AI domains. To ensure academic integrity and in accordance to the MRes degree regulations, we require MRes students to disclose AI use in this section. 

We emphasise that the MRes student (you) is responsible for the contents of their submitted thesis. Consequently, a substantial falsehood, instance of plagiarism, or misrepresentation produced by an LLM, will be regarded as academic misconduct. In such case, the penalty that the MRes student will receive is subject to Academic Misconduct Policy and Procedure at Imperial College London, which can range from re-submission to zero mark of the thesis.

Below is a list of common research subtasks along with the necessity of disclosing them. The list is adopted from ICLR 2027 author guidelines, which represents the latest guides from prestigious AI/ML conferences: \\
https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors

\paragraph{Required disclosure:} Generate synthetic data sets, help develop theoretical models or conceptual frameworks, formulate mathematical claims, provide critical ingredients for proving mathematical claims, assist in the writing of proofs, propose or refine hypotheses, design or provide feedback on research  methodology or experiments, implement methods, assist with translation, clean and reformat dataset, support qualitative and thematic data analysis, interpret results. 

\paragraph{Recommended disclosure:} Formulate questions for surveys or interviews, create or modify scientific figures or images, suggest experimental parameters, create or edit software code,  creation of artifacts, draft parts of a research paper, transcribe recordings of research material, summarize or analyse existing literature, discover research topics or identify gaps, brainstorming, sourcing/searching for information, edit a research paper to improve readability, identify relevant literature, format references, suggest a structure for a research paper, propose a title or keywords for a research paper. \\

Please also refer to the following page for College's guidance regarding responsible use of AI tools: \\
https://www.imperial.ac.uk/admin-services/library/learning-support/generative-ai-guidance/ \\

When submitting the MRes thesis, delete the above paragraphs and adapt the text below. \\

The statement should be written in complete sentences and include the following information: 

\begin{itemize}
\item Name and version of the generative AI tool e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.
\item Publisher (name of company that provides the AI system) e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.
\item URL of the AI tool 
\item Description of AI tool use: see below example AI disclosure text
\item Confirmation that the work is your own 
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Example AI disclosure description} 

Adapt below text in an appropriate way that suits your project. The example text is also adopted from ICLR 2027 author guidelines. \\

\emph{In this work, we used generative AI tools for [tasks with required disclosure]. We have not used generative AI tools for [other tasks with required disclosure], and [the rest of the required disclosure tasks] are not applicable to this work. Additionally, we used generative AI tools for [tasks with recommended disclosure]. }

\emph{We have reviewed all AI-assisted work. [Elaborate. For example, “we checked LLM-generated research ideas for potential plagiarism through a manual literature survey”, “LLM-generated code was verified and tested for correctness by 2 authors”, etc.]. We take responsibility for the final content of this work, including text, claims or artefacts produced with the aid of generative AI. }

\section{Ethical Considerations}
If your MRes thesis contain research results that require ethics approval, you are required to state relevant information in this section.

Please also detail any ethical risks considered as part of the project and the actions taken. This should follow on from the ethics review you conducted with your supervisor earlier in the project. If there are no ethical issues associated with the project, this should be stated. 

When appropriate, you are also encourage to state the broader impact of your research, including potential misuse of the research methodology.

\section{Availability of Data and Materials}
We generally expect access to be given to raw data, training materials, source code, and the like. For source code, this will usually take the form of a link to the repo you’ve used throughout development. In other situations, it may be easier to use a shared drive. Please include a statement confirming how to access data and materials. If these are private, state this. If all materials are already included in the report, please say this. If you have produced a web-app, provide a link for this to be run in production, along with any account access details that are necessary.

Please state in this section whether your research includes use of personal data or private/proprietary data. See https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/personal-information-what-is-it/what-is-personal-data/what-is-personal-data/ for more details regarding personal data.
